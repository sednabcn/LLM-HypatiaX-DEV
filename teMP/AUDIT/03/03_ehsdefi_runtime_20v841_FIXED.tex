\paragraph{Note on M3 noiseless cost.}
The 841\,s average at $\sigma=0$ was previously described as a one-time
exhaustive symbolic search cost, not a production inference time, with the
implication that modern multi-core hardware would bring it down substantially.
That explanation is now in question: raw provenance for the \emph{same}
method under the \emph{same} nominal condition (Table~\ref{tab:overall}, EHD
row) measures $7.87\pm4.88$\,s across 180 equation-runs, i.e.\ two orders of
magnitude below 841\,s -- too large a gap to attribute to a hardware
difference alone. Until the noise-sweep run's own raw log is recovered, treat
841.4\,s as an open discrepancy rather than an explained one.
