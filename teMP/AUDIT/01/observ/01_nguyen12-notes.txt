Nice catch — I recounted against your own table and the arithmetic really doesn't hold up in three separate places at once. Here's what's actually going on and how I'd fix it.
What's actually broken
1. The row totals don't match the bolded cells that are already in the table.
Counting \textbf{} cells in the extrap columns:
    • P (PySR-only): N‑1, N‑2, N‑5, N‑6, N‑8, N‑9, N‑10, N‑11 → 8/12, not the printed 10/12.
    • H (HypatiaX): N‑1, N‑2, N‑3, N‑5, N‑6, N‑8, N‑9, N‑10, N‑11 → 9 cells are bolded, not the printed 11/12.
2. Two of HypatiaX's bolded cells fail the table's own stated criterion.
The caption defines success as extrap R² ≥ 0.9999. But N‑3 = 0.9976 and N‑10 = 0.9997 are bolded anyway — both below threshold. Once you drop those two mis‑bolded cells, H's real total is 7/12, not 9 and not 11.
3. The caption has a third, independently wrong number.
Strict recovery (≥0.9999) yields 4/12 (33.3%) doesn't match 8/12, 7/12, 10/12, or 11/12 — it's not a rounding-convention artifact of any of the other numbers, it's just a fourth, disconnected figure. It reads like leftover text from an earlier draft of the paper that was never reconciled after the table was edited.
Two tiers of fix
Tier 1 — mechanical, doable right now from the table as given:
    • Un-bold N‑3 and N‑10 in the HypatiaX column (they fail the stated ≥0.9999 test).
    • Change the Success row to P: 8/12 (66.7%) and H: 7/12 (58.3%).
    • Delete the caption's 4/12 (33.3%) sentence — nothing in the table supports it, and inventing a reconciliation for it would just be a fourth guess.
Tier 2 — needs the actual data, not just re-tallying:
The per-equation R² values themselves (0.9976, 0.9997, etc.) haven't been checked against the raw results — they could be transcription errors too. Normally you'd regenerate tab:nguyen12 straight from the exp3/exp3b JSON outputs (seed=42, extended seeds 99/123/777/2024), but run_all.sh's FIX NO-TABLES-FIGURES change means the pipeline no longer emits any .tex table at all — table generation was deliberately stripped out, and only .json/.csv numerical outputs are produced now. So the table in the paper is currently hand-maintained, disconnected from the pipeline that's supposed to be its source of truth. To really close this out, someone needs to pull RESULTS_DIR/extrapolation/*.json (exp3) directly and recompute the whole table from scratch — re-tallying the printed cells (Tier 1) fixes the visible inconsistency but doesn't confirm the underlying numbers are right.

Uncovered data inconsistencies and flagged critical integrity concerns
I ran the numbers from the actual raw JSON, and this is bigger than an arithmetic bug — the source data doesn't just fail to match the printed table, it contradicts it outright, and in a way that looks like the same leak class already documented in run_all.sh.
What I found in the JSON
1. HypatiaX and PySR-only are byte-identical in every single result, in every file.
Across exp3_nguyen12_seed42.json and all four extended-seed files (99/123/777/2024) and _merged.json, the hypatiax and pysr entries have the exact same expression string and the exact same R² to 15 decimal places, for all 12 equations, every time. Independent symbolic-regression runs don't converge to bit-identical floating-point constants — this is either a copy/overwrite bug in how results get saved, or both arms are being fed the same shortcut.
2. The stored R² values contradict the published table.
I confirmed the stored evaluation.r2 field really is the extrapolation R² (recomputed it independently from X_extrap/y_extrap for N1 and got a matching ~0.99999999997). But where the paper reports catastrophic extrapolation failures — N‑3: P=‑426.2, H=0.9976; N‑4: both ≪‑100; N‑12: both negative — the raw data shows near-1.0 extrap R² across the board for both systems, including N‑3 recovering the exact symbolic ground truth (x⁵+x⁴+x³+x²+x, difference = 0 after expansion) for both H and P.
3. exp3 isn't in the FIX CRITICAL-GT-LEAK list.
run_all.sh documents ground-truth leaking into LLM prompts in three locations, explicitly scoped to exp1, exp1b, and hybrid_all_domains, and says those must be rerun before any number is cited again. exp3 (Nguyen-12, use_llm=true) was never audited for this — and the symptom here (suspiciously perfect symbolic recovery, identical across arms) is the same signature.
4. _merged.json doesn't even agree with exp3_nguyen12_seed42.json.
Both claim to represent comparable results, but the recovered expressions for N1 differ between the two files entirely, while both still show the H≡P duplication pattern. So there isn't even a single trustworthy "seed 42" source right now.
What this means for the fix
I'm not going to launder a probable data-corruption/leak artifact into the paper as a shiny "12/12, 100% success" table — that would be worse than the arithmetic bug it's replacing. So I've updated the .tex with the Tier‑1 mechanical fix (bolding corrected, row counts re-tallied to 8/12 / 7/12, stray caption number removed) and added a visible audit note flagging the new finding as blocking, rather than silently overwriting per-equation cells with numbers I don't trust.
